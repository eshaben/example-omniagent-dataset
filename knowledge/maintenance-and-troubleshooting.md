# Ridgeway Cycles Maintenance and Troubleshooting Guide

## Routine maintenance schedule

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every ride | Check tire pressure and brakes |
| Every 100 miles | Clean and lube the chain |
| Every 500 miles | Check brake pad wear and spoke tension |
| Every 1,500 miles or 12 months | Full tune-up at a Ridgeway store ($119, free for the first tune-up within 6 months of purchase) |
| Every 3,000 miles | Replace the chain (mid-drive bikes: every 2,000 miles) |

### Recommended tire pressure

| Bike | PSI |
|---|---|
| Metro 2 | 50–65 |
| Summit | 22–30 (lower for loose terrain) |
| Haul | 35–45 (use the high end when carrying passengers) |

## Battery care

- Store the battery between 40% and 80% charge if you won't ride for more than 2 weeks.
- Charge at room temperature, between 50°F and 77°F (10°C and 25°C).
- Don't leave the battery on the charger for more than 24 hours after it's full.
- In freezing weather, bring the battery indoors and let it warm up for an hour before charging.

## Troubleshooting

### The display won't turn on

1. Make sure the battery is fully seated and locked. You should hear a click.
2. Press the battery's own power button. Its LEDs show the charge level.
3. If the battery LEDs light up but the display stays off, hold the display's power button for 10 seconds to reset it.
4. If nothing lights up, charge the battery for at least 30 minutes and try again.

### Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Speed sensor not detected | Check that the wheel magnet is aligned within 5 mm of the sensor on the chainstay |
| E03 | Throttle stuck or faulty | Release the throttle and restart the bike. If it repeats, book a service visit |
| E07 | Battery temperature out of range | Stop riding, let the battery reach room temperature |
| E10 | Motor overheat | Reduce assist level and let the motor cool for 15 minutes |
| E21 | Communication error between display and controller | Restart the bike. If it repeats, book a service visit |

### Range is lower than expected

Range drops in cold weather (up to 30% below 40°F / 4°C), with low tire pressure, heavy loads, steep climbs, and high assist levels. Using Eco mode on flat sections gives the most range.

### The motor cuts out at a certain speed

This is expected. Pedal assist stops at the bike's class limit: 20 mph for Class 1 and 2, 28 mph for Class 3.

### Brakes squeal

Clean the rotors with isopropyl alcohol. If squealing continues, the pads may be contaminated with oil and need replacement.

## When to book a service visit

Book a visit for any repeating error code, a battery that won't charge, unusual motor noise, a bent rotor, or suspension that loses air. Ridgeway stores offer same-week appointments in most locations.
