# Ridgeway Cycles Shipping, Delivery, and Store Locations

## Shipping

| Region | Standard shipping | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Continental US | Free on bikes, $9 on accessories under $75 | 5–8 business days |
| Alaska and Hawaii | $199 per bike | 10–14 business days |
| Canada | Free on bikes, CA$12 on accessories under CA$100 | 7–10 business days |

Ridgeway doesn't ship outside the US and Canada.

Bikes arrive about 90% assembled. Assembly takes around 30 minutes with the included tools. Customers within 25 miles of a Ridgeway store can choose **in-store pickup** instead; the store fully assembles and tunes the bike at no cost.

### Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `processing` | Payment confirmed, preparing the order |
| `shipped` | Handed to the carrier; a tracking number is available |
| `out_for_delivery` | Arriving today |
| `delivered` | Delivered to the shipping address |
| `ready_for_pickup` | Assembled and waiting at the chosen store |

### Service types

| Service | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| `tune_up` | 1 day | $119 |
| `warranty_repair` | 1–3 days | Free (approved claims only) |
| `brake_service` | Same day | $69 per wheel |
| `flat_repair` | 30 minutes | $25 |
| `battery_diagnostic` | 1 hour | Free |
| `test_ride` | 45 minutes | Free |

Test rides need a valid driver's license or government ID and closed-toe shoes.
