# Ridgeway Cycles Warranty and Returns Policy

This policy applies to bikes and accessories bought from ridgewaycycles.example or a Ridgeway store in the United States and Canada.

## Warranty coverage

| Component | Coverage period |
|---|---|
| Frame and fork (rigid) | Lifetime, original owner |
| Suspension fork and rear shock | 2 years |
| Motor and controller | 3 years |
| R-Series battery | 2 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first |
| Display, lights, and wiring | 2 years |
| Wear parts (tires, tubes, brake pads, chain, cassette, grips) | 90 days |

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. A battery qualifies for replacement when it holds less than 70% of its original capacity within the coverage period.

### What the warranty doesn't cover

- Crash damage, racing, or jumps over 3 feet
- Damage from charging with a non-Ridgeway charger
- Water damage from submerging the bike or pressure-washing the motor or battery
- Modifications to the motor controller or speed limiter
- Normal wear on wear parts after 90 days

### How to make a warranty claim

1. Have your order number or the bike's serial number ready. The serial number is stamped under the bottom bracket.
2. Contact support by chat, phone, or email with photos of the issue.
3. If the claim is approved, support books a free service appointment at the nearest Ridgeway store, or ships a prepaid box for small parts.

Most warranty claims are decided within 3 business days.

## Returns

### 30-day ride guarantee

You can return any bike within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund if it has fewer than **100 miles** on the odometer and no damage. Ridgeway pays return shipping for bikes in the continental US and Canada.

Accessories can be returned within 30 days if unused and in original packaging. Batteries and chargers that have been used can't be returned, but they're covered by the warranty.

### Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after the returned item arrives at the warehouse. Financing (Affirm) refunds can take up to 10 business days to appear.

### Exchanges

To change size or color, start a return and place a new order. If the new bike costs less, you get the difference back; if it costs more, you pay the difference.

## Order cancellations

You can cancel an order at no cost until it ships. After it ships, use the 30-day return process.
