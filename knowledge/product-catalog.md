# Ridgeway Cycles Product Catalog (2026)

Ridgeway Cycles designs and sells electric bikes for commuting, trail riding, and cargo. Every bike ships with a Ridgeway R-Series battery, a Shimano or SRAM drivetrain, and the Ridgeway Ride app for iOS and Android.

## Commuter line

### Ridgeway Metro 2

- **SKU:** RW-MET2
- **Price:** $1,899
- **Motor:** 250 W rear hub, 45 Nm torque
- **Battery:** R-Series 418 Wh, removable
- **Range:** 35–60 miles (56–96 km) depending on assist level
- **Top assisted speed:** 20 mph (Class 1)
- **Weight:** 44 lb (20 kg)
- **Frame sizes:** S (5'0"–5'5"), M (5'5"–5'11"), L (5'11"–6'4")
- **Colors:** Graphite, Sage, Harbor Blue
- **Built-in:** front and rear lights, fenders, rear rack (55 lb capacity), kickstand

The Metro 2 is the best choice for riders who commute under 15 miles each way on paved roads.

### Ridgeway Metro 2 Step-Through

Same specs as the Metro 2 with a low step-through frame. One size fits riders 4'11" to 6'1". Colors: Graphite, Coral.

## Trail line

### Ridgeway Summit

- **SKU:** RW-SUM
- **Price:** $3,499
- **Motor:** 250 W mid-drive, 85 Nm torque
- **Battery:** R-Series 630 Wh, integrated (removable with key)
- **Range:** 25–55 miles (40–88 km) on mixed terrain
- **Top assisted speed:** 20 mph (Class 1, trail legal in most US parks)
- **Suspension:** 140 mm front fork, 130 mm rear shock
- **Weight:** 52 lb (23.6 kg)
- **Frame sizes:** S, M, L, XL
- **Colors:** Moss, Slate

The Summit is not designed for jumps over 3 feet or downhill racing. Doing so voids the frame warranty.

## Cargo line

### Ridgeway Haul

- **SKU:** RW-HAUL
- **Price:** $2,799
- **Motor:** 750 W rear hub, 80 Nm torque
- **Battery:** R-Series 720 Wh, dual-battery capable (second battery sold separately, $649)
- **Range:** 30–50 miles single battery, 60–100 miles dual
- **Top assisted speed:** 20 mph throttle, 28 mph pedal assist (Class 3 setting can be locked to Class 2)
- **Payload:** 400 lb total, including rider
- **Weight:** 68 lb (30.8 kg)
- **Frame size:** One size, riders 5'2"–6'3"
- **Accessories:** child seat mount, cargo bags, running boards

## Batteries and chargers

| Battery | Capacity | Full charge time (standard 4A charger) | Fast charger (6A) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-Series 418 | 418 Wh | 4.5 hours | 3 hours |
| R-Series 630 | 630 Wh | 6 hours | 4 hours |
| R-Series 720 | 720 Wh | 7 hours | 4.5 hours |

Batteries keep about 80% of their original capacity after 800 full charge cycles. The fast charger costs $129 and works with every R-Series battery.
